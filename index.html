Master's research

My thesis, done at the STORM Lab in Leeds, looked at chemically modified polyimide (Kapton) as a self-healing sleeve for continuum robots. Continuum robots bend along their length and are often wrapped in a thin skin. When the skin tears, the robot usually has to come out of service. The aim was a sleeve that can repair itself.
